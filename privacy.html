Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026.

This website is a static site with no backend or database. It does not collect, store, or process personal information on its own servers.

The contact form

The enquiry form on the Contact page does not submit your details to ShrikantAI Innovation directly or store them anywhere. When you submit the form, your browser prepares a WhatsApp message containing the details you entered, and opens WhatsApp for you to review and send. We only receive your information once you choose to send that message, and from that point it is handled as a normal WhatsApp conversation, subject to WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

Direct contact

If you contact us by phone, email, or WhatsApp, we use the information you share only to respond to your enquiry and discuss your project. We do not sell or share your contact details with third parties.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not use cookies or third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes as the website or our services evolve, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
