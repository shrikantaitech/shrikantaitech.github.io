Engineering better ways to work.

Most businesses lose time to manual, repetitive work — chasing payments, answering the same questions, re-entering data between tools. We design and build the software, automation, and AI agents that take that work off your plate, so your team can focus on customers and growth.

ShrikantAI Innovation works with clinics, service businesses, and growing SMEs to replace manual processes with systems that are built around how the business actually runs — not a generic template. Every engagement starts with understanding the real problem before a single line of code is written.

What we build.

Seven areas of work, each solving a specific operational problem rather than selling a feature list.

AI Agents & Business Automation

Operations

Repetitive decisions and follow-ups — qualifying leads, sending reminders, updating records — consume staff time every day. We design AI agents and automation workflows that handle these tasks reliably, with a clear handoff to a human whenever judgment is needed.

Voice AI & Calling Solutions

Customer contact

Missed calls mean missed business, and front-desk staff can’t answer every query instantly. We build voice AI systems that handle routine calls — appointment requests, basic queries, call routing — and pass on anything that needs a person.

WhatsApp Business Automation

Customer contact

Customers already expect to reach you on WhatsApp. We build automated flows for enquiries, order updates, appointment confirmations, and support — connected to your existing records, so replies are accurate and timely without someone manually typing every message.

CRM & Workflow Automation

Sales & operations

Leads and tasks fall through the cracks when they’re tracked across spreadsheets, chats, and memory. We set up CRM systems and workflow automation that keep every lead, task, and follow-up visible and on schedule.

Healthcare & Clinic Software

Healthcare

Clinics juggle appointments, patient records, and billing, often across paper and disconnected tools. We build clinic management software suited to how a clinic actually works day to day, reducing double entry and missed appointments.

Custom Web Application Development

Software

Off-the-shelf software rarely fits a specific business process exactly. We design and build custom web applications — internal tools, customer portals, booking systems — shaped around your actual workflow rather than forcing you into someone else’s.

API Integration & Technology Consulting

Infrastructure

Payment gateways, messaging platforms, CRMs, and accounting tools need to talk to each other, and most businesses don’t have the in-house expertise to connect them safely. We handle the integration work and advise on the right technology choices for where your business actually is.
