About

A Pune-based technology initiative.

Our vision.

ShrikantAI Innovation is a Pune-based technology initiative focused on practical AI and custom software engineering. We want businesses of every size to use intelligent systems without needing a large technology team.

We help businesses and clinics simplify operations, improve customer communication and build digital systems around the way they already work.

Practical AI engineering.

We use AI where it removes real work: answering routine questions, capturing enquiries, routing calls and messages, and keeping follow-ups from slipping. Where a person should decide, the system hands over to a person.

That is how we approach AI agent development, voice AI and WhatsApp automation: small, understandable systems that your team can trust and adjust.
