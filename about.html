About ShrikantAI Innovation

A Pune-based technology initiative focused on practical AI and software solutions for growing businesses.

ShrikantAI Innovation builds AI agents, automation systems, and custom software for businesses that need technology to fit their actual workflow — not the other way around. The work spans clinics, service businesses, and growing SMEs across Pune and beyond, with a focus on systems that get used, not just demoed.

How we think about the work

Technology is only useful if it solves a real operational problem and fits into how a business already runs. That means starting every engagement by understanding the business first — its process, its tools, its team — before proposing any technology. It also means being direct about what a system can and can’t do, rather than overselling capability.

Our principles

  • Start with the business problem, not the technology.
  • Build systems people will actually use day to day.
  • Integrate with the tools a business already relies on.
  • Communicate directly, without layers of account management.
  • Be honest about project status and capability — no inflated claims.
  • Stay involved after launch, not just at delivery.
